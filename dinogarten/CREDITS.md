The dinosaur models and their animations are by [Quaternius](https://quaternius.com), released into the public domain under [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). Thank you!

| File | Model | Downloaded from |

The models are used unchanged. Their colours, cartoon eyes, proportions and smooth shading are applied by the game when it loads them.
